Install in one click

Connect your Flipper with a USB cable and press the button. ESL Zap detects your firmware (Official, Momentum, Unleashed, RogueMaster) and installs the matching build.

Your browser cannot talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer, or use the manual download below.
  1. Connect

    Pick your Flipper in the browser window. Close qFlipper first.

  2. Detect firmware

    ESL Zap reads the firmware name and API version.

  3. Copy the app

    Saved to Apps, Infrared, with an images folder ready to use.

From photo to label

Add your label

In the app open My tags and type digits 3 to 16 of the label barcode (the first two and the last one are not needed), or import the label from NFC if it has a chip. ESL Zap recognises the model.

Pick a picture

Prepare it here and press Copy to my Flipper, or drop any .bmp into apps_data/esl_zap/images on the SD card.

Aim and send

Hold the Flipper 5 to 30 cm from the label with the IR window facing it and press Send.

Wait a moment

The label wakes up, receives the image and redraws its screen a few seconds later.

Manual download

Each firmware needs its own build. Pick yours, copy the file with qFlipper to SD Card/apps/Infrared and start it from Apps on the Flipper.

Before you start

Never remove the battery of a label: most models keep their address and firmware in volatile memory and stop working for good.

ESL Zap talks to infrared labels only. Labels updated by radio (most new store systems) cannot be changed.

Use ESL Zap only on labels you own. Changing displays in shops is not allowed.
